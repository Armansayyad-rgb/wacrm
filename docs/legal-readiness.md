# Global legal and policy readiness

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice. Final customer-facing terms should be reviewed for the operator's actual legal entity and launch jurisdictions.

## Required before public paid launch

- Choose the legal entity or individual that operates the service.
- Publish a Privacy Policy that identifies the operator and privacy contact.
- Publish Terms of Service that identify the contracting party, acceptable use, subscription terms, suspension/termination rules, liability terms, and governing law.
- Publish a clear anti-spam / acceptable-use rule for WhatsApp messaging.
- Document customer responsibilities for WhatsApp recipient consent, templates, and Meta policies.
- Document subprocessors used by the service, including hosting/database, Paddle, Meta/WhatsApp, and any optional AI provider selected by the customer.
- Provide a data-deletion/contact path for account owners.
- Decide retention periods for messages, contacts, logs, webhook events, and deleted workspaces.
- Confirm how backups are deleted after account deletion.
- Add a DPA process for business customers that require one.
- Review international data-transfer requirements for the hosting regions selected at launch.

## Billing disclosure

Paddle is intended to be the payment and transaction Merchant of Record. The software should still disclose the operator of the service and should not imply that Paddle replaces every corporate, income-tax, privacy, employment, or regulatory obligation of the operator.

## Product disclosure

WhatsApp is a Meta product. The service should state that it is not endorsed by or affiliated with Meta unless an approved partnership status is actually obtained.

## Information still required from the operator

The repository intentionally does not invent these values:

- permanent product name
- legal operator/entity name
- registered/business address where legally required
- support email
- privacy contact email
- governing law / jurisdiction
- production domain
- selected Supabase project region

Fill those values only after they are genuinely chosen and verified.
